# Phase 1: Business Understanding & Problem Statement

## Executive Summary
Customer churn is one of the most critical challenges facing telecommunication service providers. Acquiring a new customer costs 5 to 25 times more than retaining an existing one. Reducing churn by just 5% can increase profits by 25% to 95%.

This project delivers an enterprise-grade **Customer Churn Prediction & Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) Engine** to enable proactive customer retention, identify high-value at-risk subscribers, and optimize marketing intervention strategies.

---

## Business Problem Statement
The telecommunications company is experiencing customer loss across multiple contract types, payment channels, and service packages. Without a predictive capability, retention teams rely on reactive discounts or uniform outreach after customers signal intent to leave, leading to wasted spend and diminished ROI.

### Core Business Questions:
1. Which customers are at high risk of churning within the next billing cycle?
2. What are the key underlying drivers influencing a customer's decision to churn?
3. What is the expected Lifetime Value (LTV) of each customer, and how much revenue is at risk?
4. How can retention campaigns be targeted dynamically based on risk tier and customer valuation?

---

## Strategic Objectives
1. **Predictive Accuracy**: Build high-performing classification models (Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, XGBoost) to achieve an ROC-AUC $\ge 0.84$ and recall $\ge 0.75$ for churn detection.
2. **Explainability**: Utilize SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to provide interpretable feature contributions for individual customer predictions.
3. **Customer Lifetime Value Estimation**: Compute historical and predicted LTV using retention probability, monthly charges, and tenure duration.
4. **Operationalization**: Deploy a low-latency FastAPI backend microservice for real-time inference and batch scoring.
5. **Business Intelligence**: Deliver an interactive Power BI dashboard featuring executive KPIs, churn breakdowns, financial risk heatmaps, and action triggers.

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Churn Rate (%)**: Percentage of total customer base terminating service within a month.
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) at Risk**: Sum of monthly charges for high-risk churn customers.
- **Model Recall (Sensitivity)**: Proportion of actual churners correctly identified by the engine.
- **Model Precision**: Proportion of predicted churners who actually churn.
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Estimated net revenue generated by a customer over their entire lifespan.
  $$\text{Baseline LTV} = \text{MonthlyCharges} \times \text{Tenure}$$
  $$\text{Predicted LTV} = \text{MonthlyCharges} \times \left(\text{Tenure} + \frac{1}{\text{Churn Probability} + \epsilon}\right)$$
- **Customer Retention ROI**: Net financial gain from retention campaigns divided by campaign cost.

---

## Target Audience & Operational Use Cases
- **Customer Success & Retention Teams**: Prioritize daily outreach lists based on Churn Risk Score and LTV rank.
- **Marketing Strategy Leaders**: Target specialized incentives (e.g., free tech support, contract upgrade discounts) to high-value, high-risk cohorts.
- **Executive Leadership**: Monitor enterprise MRR stability, churn trajectory, and retention campaign performance via Power BI dashboards.
